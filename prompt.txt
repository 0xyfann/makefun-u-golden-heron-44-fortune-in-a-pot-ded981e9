Write one kind, funny fortune (max 120 characters, one line) for a
houseplant on the windowsill, as if the plant itself were having its
future told. It must sound like a fortune — a prediction, not a slogan,
caption, or care instruction — be warm with no doom or mockery, and feel
written for this specific plant in this window, not any plant anywhere.
The small joke should land for a plant lover: the keeper proudly showing
the plant off to guests. No puns that groan, no padding to reach the
character limit.
